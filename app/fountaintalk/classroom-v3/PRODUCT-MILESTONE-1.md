## Product principles enforced

- One clear primary action per screen
- No onboarding interview
- No “mentor” title in learner-facing copy
- No course-catalog language
- Journeys and episodes instead of lessons and modules
- Reflection captures learning naturally
- Profile emphasizes remembered ideas over scores and streaks
